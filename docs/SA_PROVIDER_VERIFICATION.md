## Regulatory warning
The National Gambling Board states that interactive online casino gambling is prohibited while appropriately licensed online betting is treated differently. Its published 2025/2026 positions raise particular risk for remote casino-style games, even where a bookmaker offers them. The legal status of free-play demonstrations requires specific advice; do not assume a demo exemption.
