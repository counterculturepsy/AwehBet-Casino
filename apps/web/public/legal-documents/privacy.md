# AwehBet Privacy Policy

**Status: Development draft — not yet in force**  
**Effective date:** To be confirmed after legal and regulatory approval  
**Last updated:** 16 October 2026

## 1. Who we are

AwehBet is the trading name used by the development platform at **awehbet.co.za**. The full registered operator name, company registration number and registered address have not yet been confirmed for publication. No real-money service may be activated until those details and the responsible legal entity have been verified.

This policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, protect and retain personal information about website visitors, applicants for accounts and customers. It also explains your privacy choices and how to contact us. It is intended to operate alongside our Terms and Conditions, Responsible Gambling Policy and Cookie Notice.

Our privacy practices are subject to the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA) and other applicable South African laws. This policy is a notice about processing; visiting the website or accepting our terms does not, by itself, constitute consent to every use of your information.

**Information Officer:** To be designated before production processing

## 2. Information we collect

We collect information relevant to the service you request and the checks we must perform. Depending on your interactions with us, this may include:

| Category | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| Identity and eligibility | Full name, date of birth, ID or passport details, nationality and copies of verification documents. |
| Contact and residence | Email address, mobile number, residential address and proof of address. |
| Account information | Account identifier, login and authentication records, account status and communication preferences. |
| Payment and financial information | Bank account details, payment references, deposits, withdrawals, refunds, chargebacks and payment verification results. Source-of-funds or source-of-wealth information may be required for applicable checks. |
| Gambling activity | Bets, game sessions, stakes, results, winnings, bonuses, balances and transaction history. |
| Responsible gambling | Limits, time-outs, self-exclusion requests, relevant activity indicators and information you provide when seeking support. |
| Technical and usage information | IP address, device and browser information, access times, pages visited, security events and approximate location inferred from an IP address. |
| Communications | Support messages, complaints, requests and related correspondence. Calls are recorded only where recording is used and an appropriate notice has been provided. |

Payment providers may collect information directly through their own payment pages. Their own privacy notices explain processing for which they are independently responsible. The allocation of payment information between AwehBet and each provider will be explained in the relevant payment flow.

Where a verification process involves a selfie, facial matching or other biometric information, we will provide a specific notice identifying the provider, purpose, information involved and applicable lawful authorisation before that processing occurs. We apply the additional legal requirements for special personal information, including health-related information supplied in a responsible gambling request.

## 3. Where information comes from

We receive information directly from you when you register, verify your identity, transact, use account controls or contact us. We also collect technical information through your use of our services.

Where lawful and relevant, we receive information from payment providers, identity and age verification services, fraud prevention services, game providers, authorised representatives, public registers and competent authorities. We use those sources to verify information, process transactions, investigate irregular activity and meet legal obligations.

## 4. Why we use information

We use personal information to:

- Assess applications, verify age and identity, create accounts and provide account access.
- Process deposits and withdrawals, maintain balances, settle transactions and resolve payment disputes.
- Provide games and betting services available through the platform, administer applicable bonuses and maintain accurate activity records.
- Conduct applicable customer due diligence, sanctions and anti-money-laundering checks, and make required reports.
- Detect account misuse, payment fraud, unauthorised access and other security threats.
- Apply responsible gambling controls, administer self-exclusion and respond to requests for support.
- Answer questions, investigate complaints and maintain service correspondence.
- Diagnose faults, assess service performance and improve usability using information proportionate to those purposes.
- Send essential account, security, transaction and policy notices.
- Send promotional communications where lawfully permitted and consistent with your preferences.
- Meet legal, regulatory, audit and recordkeeping duties and establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

We rely on the lawful ground appropriate to each activity: steps requested before entering a contract, performance of our contract with you, compliance with a legal duty, protection of a legitimate interest where permitted, or your consent where required. We do not treat consent as the basis for processing that the law requires us to perform.

Information required to verify eligibility, satisfy applicable legal duties or process a transaction is mandatory for the relevant service. If you do not provide it, we may be unable to open an account, provide that service or complete the transaction. Optional marketing choices are voluntary.

## 5. Who receives information

We disclose relevant information only where there is a lawful purpose and appropriate protection. Recipients may include:

- Banks, payment processors and providers supporting deposits, withdrawals and payment disputes.
- Verification, fraud prevention and compliance providers.
- Game and platform providers requiring information to deliver and reconcile activity.
- Hosting, infrastructure, security, communications and customer support providers.
- Responsible gambling services or exclusion registers where disclosure is authorised or required.
- Professional advisers and auditors subject to appropriate confidentiality duties.
- The Financial Intelligence Centre, relevant gambling authorities, law enforcement, courts and other competent bodies where disclosure is required or otherwise lawful.

Providers acting on our instructions must be subject to written obligations covering confidentiality, security and authorised processing. Some recipients, including banks and regulators, process information under their own legal responsibilities.

If our business is restructured, sold or merged, relevant information may be disclosed to advisers and a prospective or actual successor, subject to lawful grounds, appropriate confidentiality and any required notice.

## 6. Marketing and cookies

We send electronic marketing only with valid consent or where an applicable legal exception permits it, including the limited existing-customer exception for our own similar services when its requirements are met. Marketing communications identify us and provide a straightforward way to opt out. You can also contact our privacy email to change your preferences without charge.

Opting out of promotions does not stop essential service, transaction, security or legally required messages. Self-exclusion and other applicable responsible gambling restrictions take priority over marketing preferences.

We use cookies or similar technologies where required to support functions such as login sessions, security and saved preferences. Any analytics, advertising or other optional tracking actually deployed must be described in our Cookie Notice, including provider, purpose, duration and available controls. Where consent is required, optional tracking is used only after that consent. You can also manage cookies through your browser; blocking necessary cookies may affect account functions.

**Cookie Notice:** To be published before optional analytics or advertising technologies are enabled.

## 7. Transfers outside South Africa

Some service providers may process information outside South Africa. We make such transfers only where a condition under section 72 of POPIA is satisfied, for example appropriate protection through applicable law or a binding agreement, valid consent, or another permitted ground. Additional restrictions applicable to special personal information and children's information must also be met.

**Overseas processing relevant to our service:** No production provider set has been approved. Countries, recipient categories, purposes and section 72 safeguards must be documented before production processing begins. Contact details for the Information Officer will be published once designated.

## 8. How long we retain information

We retain information for the period needed for its specified purpose or a lawful retention requirement. Account closure does not automatically erase records that must be retained.

Where the Financial Intelligence Centre Act applies, customer due diligence records relating to establishing a business relationship must generally be kept for at least five years after that relationship ends; transaction records for at least five years after the transaction; and records of activity giving rise to a section 29 report for at least five years after the report is submitted. Other laws, licence requirements, investigations or legal holds may require longer retention.

Responsible gambling and exclusion records may need to be retained to continue enforcing restrictions. Other account, support, technical and marketing records follow our documented retention schedule. When continued retention is no longer justified, we delete, destroy or de-identify the information appropriately.

**Retention schedule:** Category-specific periods for inactive accounts, support correspondence, technical logs and backups must be approved and published before production processing begins.

## 9. Security and security compromises

We apply reasonable technical and organisational safeguards appropriate to the information and risks involved. Access must be limited to authorised persons who need information for their work, and providers must protect information entrusted to them. We review safeguards as risks and services change.

If there are reasonable grounds to believe that an unauthorised person has accessed or acquired personal information under our control, we will notify the Information Regulator and affected people as soon as reasonably possible, subject to POPIA's permitted qualifications. We will provide relevant information about the incident, protective action and steps affected people can take.

Keep your login details private and contact support promptly if you suspect unauthorised access.

## 10. Automated assessments

Automated tools may support verification, fraud monitoring and identification of potentially harmful gambling patterns, where those tools are deployed. Such assessments can flag activity for review or affect access to a transaction or account function.

**Actual automated processing:** The development platform performs format, eligibility and duplicate-account checks. It does not currently make a solely automated decision with a legal or similarly substantial effect. Any future fraud, verification or safer-gambling profiling must be documented with its inputs, consequences and human-review route before activation. Decisions based solely on automated profiling that have legal or substantial effects must comply with section 71 of POPIA.

## 11. Your privacy rights

Subject to applicable legal requirements and exceptions, you may:

- Ask whether we hold personal information about you and request access to it.
- Request correction or deletion of information that is inaccurate, irrelevant, excessive, outdated, incomplete, misleading or unlawfully obtained, or destruction of records we may no longer retain.
- Object to processing where POPIA allows an objection, including direct marketing.
- Withdraw consent for future processing that depends on it, without affecting earlier lawful processing.
- Exercise applicable rights relating to significant automated decisions.
- Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator.

Send requests to our Information Officer using the contact details above. We may request proportionate proof of identity to prevent unauthorised disclosure. Confirmation of whether we hold information is free; access fees may apply only where legally permitted and will be explained in advance. We will explain any lawful limitation or refusal and the available route to challenge it.

Prescribed POPIA forms and, where applicable, our PAIA Manual may assist with a request. **PAIA Manual:** To be published where applicable before production launch.

## 12. People under 18

AwehBet's gambling services are intended only for people aged 18 or older. We do not offer gambling accounts to children. If you believe a child has supplied information to us, contact our Information Officer. We will investigate, restrict any affected account and handle the information in accordance with applicable law, including any duty to preserve relevant records.

## 13. Complaints

You can raise a privacy concern with our Information Officer. You may also complain directly to the Information Regulator (South Africa); you do not have to obtain our permission or complete our internal process first.

**Information Regulator complaints and current submission channels:** https://inforegulator.org.za/complaints/

## 14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our services, processing activities or legal obligations change. The current version will appear on our website with an updated date. We will give appropriate notice of material changes and obtain fresh consent where required. A policy update does not itself authorise a new use of information.
